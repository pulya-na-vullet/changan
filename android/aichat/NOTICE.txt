RHVoice speech engine (LGPL-2.1+)
  https://github.com/RHVoice/RHVoice
  Native library: libRHVoice_jni.so from the F-Droid RHVoice APK
  (com.github.olga_yakovleva.rhvoice.android). Java JNI wrappers are
  from RHVoice-core.

Elena Russian voice (GPL-3.0)
  https://github.com/RHVoice/elena-rus
  Bundled under assets/rhvoice/voices/elena and languages/Russian.

AI Chat loads RHVoice itself and plays PCM with AudioTrack. It does not
use Android's TextToSpeech API, so Feiyu does not need a system TTS engine.
